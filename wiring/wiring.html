Ball balancer wiring

Every connection, generated from the same list as the tables below (wiring/wiring.py). Power and ground run through eight WAGO 221-415 lever connectors: two for +12 V, two for +5 V, four for ground, each pair or group linked by a short jumper. Signals are Dupont leads. Touch panel, buttons and soft power

Before you power it on

  • Fit the motors' leads last. First power-up: no motors connected, check 12 V on the +12 V Wagos and 5.0 V on the +5 V Wagos with a meter.
  • Never connect the buck's 5 V output and the Mega's USB 5 V together; the Mega takes 12 V on VIN only and makes its own 5 V.
  • Touch-panel tail order (Y−, X−, Y+, X+) is from a similar panel's datasheet: confirm with a meter (about 200–900 Ω across each X pair and each Y pair).
  • Motor connector pins, left to right: 1 M+, 2 GND, 3 A, 4 B, 5 5V, 6 M−. Port labels give the pin number.
  • Our motor cable colours: M+ yellow, GND black, A blue, B purple, 5V red, M− white.
  • Our button harness colours: NO blue, C green, NC yellow, LED+ red, LED− black.
  • Button harness colours vary by batch: identify LED+, LED−, C, NO and NC with a meter before connecting.
  • Program the Mega over USB with the shell lifted; the soft switch can stay off while USB powers the Mega's logic.
  • Second MDD3A's channel 2 is unused. Leave both MDD3As' 5V pins unconnected: they are outputs from the board's own regulator.
